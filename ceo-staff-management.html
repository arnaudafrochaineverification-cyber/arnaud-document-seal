Staff Roles

  • Country Manager: supervises authorised operations in an assigned country.
  • Verification Officer: handles assigned document-review tasks.
  • Customer Support: assists clients with permitted account enquiries.
  • CEO: controls staff assignments and recruitment approvals.
These roles must be enforced by Firebase Security Rules and trusted server-side functions. A role label in a profile is not sufficient by itself to grant secure access.
