Staff Roles & Responsibilities

Director Manager

Oversees company operations, management coordination, reporting and approved strategic activities.

Country Manager

Coordinates approved operations within an assigned country and reports through the authorised management structure.

Branch Manager

Coordinates branch operations, staff activities and service delivery within the approved branch.

Verification Officer

Handles authorised document verification tasks and follows company verification procedures.

Administrative Officer

Supports records, communication, scheduling and routine administrative processes.

Customer Support Officer

Assists customers with enquiries and routes verification or application concerns to the correct department.

Human Resources Officer

Supports recruitment coordination, staff records and approved personnel processes.

These descriptions are informational. Actual authority must be assigned and enforced through the company's approved access rules.
